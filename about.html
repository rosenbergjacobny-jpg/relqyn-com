The Relqyn story

We’re here for the unglamorous rituals.

Dish days. Spill days. The “where did all the towels go?” days. These are the moments our homes are made of, and they deserve products that work beautifully without adding to the pile of things we throw away.

Relqyn creates practical kitchen essentials with a preference for plant-based materials, reusable formats, and packaging that keeps its footprint light. We focus on the details—how it feels in your hand, how it lives under the sink, and whether you’ll reach for it again tomorrow.
